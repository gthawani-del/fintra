## Pipeline

Approved dialogue → Hindi speech files and measured timings → amplitude and subtitle cues → code-drawn scene animation → official Lemo still checks → official Lemo full render → original Python score and ducking → FFmpeg loudness/mux → encoded-file QA. No paid tool calls. No image model or Luma used.

## Limits

Speech-amplitude mouth movement is not phoneme lip sync. Some opening subtitle timings are proportional to sentence lengths because the approved audio has no word boundary metadata. The artwork is an illustrated motion style, not hyperrealistic film. Numerical quote is a story example, not financial advice.
