## Structure selection
1. Three separate mini-stories: clear but emotionally fragmented.
2. Narrated list of everyday AI uses: simple but repeats the rejected explainer framing.
3. One family across dinner, the following day, and dinner again: chosen. Their differing attitudes create humour and interdependence.

Logline: A worried administrator and his uncertain college-age daughter discover that the mother already uses AI for her home-food business; a faulty quotation brings their abilities together.
Setup: AI means fear, uncertainty, or opportunity. Turn: a polished answer conceals missing costs. Ending: they work together, with no promise of job security or instant success.

## Visual benchmark and adaptation
Learn from UPA-era limited animation: expressive held poses, colour planes and readable silhouettes; from domestic situation comedy: reaction pauses, a consistent room, dialogue crossing cuts. Do not copy characters, shots, jokes, music or designs.
Custom adaptation of Mid-century Cartoon: contemporary Indian home, consistent natural skin tones, no script lettering or classroom step medallions. Paper pop-up metaphor and desktop composition complement the family world. All drawings authored in Canvas; no stock footage, generated video or photographs.
