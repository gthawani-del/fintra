# Ramayana — three-medium narrated proof

Approved scope: 45–60 seconds, Ayodhya in moving oil paint; dramatic exchange in shadow theatre; Hanuman crossing the ocean in layered paper. Hindi narrator, 16:9. Duration target 54 seconds. No paid generative-media calls.

## Candidate structures and choice
1. Continuous chronological micro-retelling: compresses too much narrative into 54 seconds.
2. Three disconnected demonstrations: makes the material clear but loses emotional continuity.
3. **Chosen: promise → sacrifice → courage.** Three selected moments linked by the narration, clearly an excerpt montage, not a complete chronology.

Opening: morning light on Ayodhya and a court-bound Rama. Ending: Hanuman crossing toward the distant island, carrying a message of hope. Titles identify the three places/moments. No fabricated quotation attributed to scripture; narration and dramatic dialogue are original paraphrases.

## Shot plan
0–5: lateral wide reveal of warm Ayodhya, Sarayu, painted architecture and flags. Establish beauty and scale.
5–11: closer palace view, Rama turns toward the court; cloth and bow move with him. Introduce the person behind the duty.
11–17: Rama releases his hand from the idea of kingship and turns away; paint-over transition to cloth theatre.
17–24: Kaikeyi and Dasharatha in opposing profiles; Kaikeyi raises her hand while narrator states the two demands. Palace arches, glowing translucent dyed puppet pieces.
24–30: Dasharatha recoils and lowers his head; the crown sinks in his hand. Give consequence time to read.
30–35: Rama enters, bows, and resolves to leave. Theatre light narrows into a paper moon.
35–40: layered ocean/cliffs, Hanuman prepares, bends knees, lifts gaze.
40–46: leap, close tracking silhouette, separate limbs, scarf and tail follow through; foreground waves move faster than horizon.
46–51: pull out to ocean scale, Hanuman crossing toward distant Lanka.
51–54: hopeful hold with continuing waves, scarf and light; short end title.

## Look / adaptations
Use Lemo impasto renderer for palette-knife paint with fixed stroke seeds, custom Indian architecture and character design. Use transmitted-light puppet construction inspired by Lemo shadow-puppet mechanics, explicitly adapted to Indian ornament and costume. Paper-cut lightbox uses flat layers, depth shadows and warm emissive light, custom ocean and characters. No claim that this is an unchanged upstream Chinese theatre style.
Consistent Rama: blue-grey skin, ochre clothing, tied dark hair, longbow. Hanuman: warm ivory figure, red-orange cloth, long curving tail. Decorative illustration, not photorealism.
Typography: Noto Serif Devanagari titles; Noto Sans Devanagari readable narration captions; no handwritten text.

## Sound
Hindi male storyteller, measured phrasing. Original synthesized plucked-string/breath-flute motif, soft hand-drum accents, cloth/wood rustle and ocean. Sound transitions lead image at 16.5 and 34.5. Near-silence after demands and before leap. Speech foremost; no chanting or unverifiable Sanskrit.
