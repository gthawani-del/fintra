# Ramayana: worlds within a promise — material transformation proof
Status: new visual direction; 24-second music-led proof, not full-film replacement.
Three structures considered:
1. Montage of unrelated style samples — rejected: gallery rather than film.
2. Repair prior puppet scenes — rejected: preserves overly formal tonality.
3. One visual motif changes material — chosen: sun / palace window / ocean horizon.
0–8s: original procedural Ayodhya palace on a river. Native impasto knife engine,
cobalt and coral planes, saffron windows, reflected dabs. Camera moves through
the painting toward its central sun.
8–15s: the circular sun becomes a crafted window with cobalt/ruby/gold glass,
material texture, dark leading, actual transmitted-light compositor. Indian
palace geometry and sun motif; no borrowed church narrative. Light and camera
carry the transition.
15–24s: move through the central circular opening into a full-bleed paper ocean.
A ladder of separate blue/teal wave sheets with warm edge light, shadows and
depth-dependent movement. Hanuman's approved red-cloth flying cutout crosses.
Palette: ultramarine #173d9d, coral #f16b59, saffron #f6b645, teal #159f9d,
ruby #9e254d, deep ink #111d38. No beige background, no brown grade.
Type: one final Hindi title cut from light, large bold; no invented spoken words.
Audio: use an excerpt of existing original instrumental score, no TTS changes.
Review: actual browser frames at transitions and motion strips, then MP4 decode.
Hybrid composition is explicitly adapted; do not claim untouched compliance with
all native style invariants where material transformations cross boundaries.
